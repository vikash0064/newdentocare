# SEO Backlink Strategy Checklist

1. Submit site to local business directories (JustDial, Practo, Google My Business, etc.)
2. Reach out to dental associations and health organizations for links.
3. Collaborate with local clinics, hospitals, and educational institutions.
4. Guest post on health blogs and dental websites.
5. Share valuable guides, infographics, and resources for others to reference.
6. Engage in forums and Q&A platforms (Quora, Reddit, etc.)
7. Request reviews on reputable platforms.
8. Track new backlinks monthly using tools like Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Google Search Console.
9. Build relationships with influencers and bloggers in the dental/health niche.
10. Monitor competitor backlinks and replicate high-quality sources.
11. Add Open Graph and Twitter meta tags for better social sharing
12. Add schema markup (JSON-LD) for local business and reviews
13. Ensure robots.txt and sitemap.xml are present and correct
14. Add AMP or optimize for mobile speed
15. Use canonical tags to avoid duplicate content
16. Compress images and optimize for faster loading
